# Demo media license

`demo.gif` and `demo.png` are adapted from the OpenMantra dataset by Ryota Hinami, Shonosuke Ishiwatari, Kazuhiko Yasuda and Yusuke Matsui ("Towards Fully Automated Manga Translation", AAAI 2021), available at https://github.com/mantra-inc/open-mantra-dataset. They show pages from the series `tencho_isoro` (`images/tencho_isoro/ja/002.jpg` and `003.jpg`).

The original work is licensed under Creative Commons Attribution-NonCommercial 4.0 International (CC BY-NC 4.0), https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc/4.0/. These files are licensed under the same terms.

Changes were made. The Japanese text was machine-translated into English, removed from the art, and re-lettered by Yomikae. The frames also show the pages inside a demo reader page with captions added.
